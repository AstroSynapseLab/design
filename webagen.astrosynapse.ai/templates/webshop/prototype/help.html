Shipping, returns and payment

How much is shipping?

Free on orders over €75,00; otherwise €4,90. Delivery takes 2–4 working days anywhere in the EU, and orders placed by 14:00 leave Zagreb the same day.

Which delivery methods can I choose?

Standard: DPD or GLS to your door. Pickup point: any DPD Pickup or Box, €2,00 less than standard. Express: next working day for €9,90, Croatia only, order by 14:00.

How do returns work?

You have 30 days to return anything for free. A prepaid label is in the box, and we refund within 5 days of receiving the parcel.

Is VAT included?

Yes. Every price includes 25% VAT, so there are no surprises at checkout.

What warranty do shoes have?

Two years on every pair, with repairs done in Zagreb.

How can I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Klarna and SEPA. Guest checkout, no account needed.
