Korvane Silicon is a fabless semiconductor design house: part chip design studio, part product company.

ST/04.1 · Practice

What we do

General-purpose chips are built to sell into every market at once, which means every product that uses them pays for features it will never touch, in power, in board space and in cost. We design silicon around a specific job and strip out everything else.

We split our work into two halves that feed each other. Design services fund our research: custom ASICs and SoCs for clients, plus verification, analog IP, packaging, test and firmware as stand-alone services. That research becomes reusable IP, which goes into our own chips (Nerva N1, Ostra P8, Lumen R60, Vault M4) and the Tessel evaluation platform. Our products prove the IP at volume before it goes back into client designs, so every block we license has already shipped in production.

We don't own a fab. We own the design, the verification, the test programs and the relationship with every foundry and packaging partner in our supply chain, so our customers get one accountable team from first specification to volume production.

Our story

Korvane Silicon was founded in 2019 by four engineers who had spent their careers inside large semiconductor companies, watching good product ideas get cancelled because they didn't fit a roadmap built for smartphones. They started Korvane to design the chips those companies wouldn't: low-volume, long-life, highly specific silicon for industrial, medical and automotive products.

The first years were funded entirely by custom ASIC work for clients. In 2022, the power management IP built for those clients became Ostra P8, our first product. Nerva N1 followed in 2024.

How we operate

Hardware punishes improvisation: a mistake found after tape-out costs months, not hours. Every program runs on a small set of internal protocols, refined across 23 tape-outs: Specification Freeze, Architecture Review, Verification Sign-off, Tape-out Readiness, Silicon Bring-up, Qualification & Release, and Customer Handoff.

The same toolchain and IP library serves our own products and our client programs: RISC-V cores, SystemVerilog and UVM, custom ADCs, PLLs and mmWave front ends, firmware in C and Rust on Zephyr. What we build for ourselves and what we build for clients stays compatible.

  • Small, senior teams: every program is led by an engineer with at least two tape-outs behind them.
  • Honest recommendations: we turn down programs where custom silicon isn't the right answer.
  • Long relationships: our first client is still a client, now on its third chip.

Supply chain & quality

The chip shortage of the early 2020s stopped production lines for want of parts costing less than a euro. Every Korvane product is designed to avoid single points of failure: two foundries per process node with at least one in Europe, two packaging and test partners on two continents, a fifteen-year longevity commitment on every standard product, and buffer stock programs in bonded storage for customers on long-term agreements.

  • ISO 9001
  • ISO 26262 (ASIL-B capable)
  • AEC-Q100
  • IEC 62443
  • wafer-to-unit traceability
  • 8D failure analysis
  • EU Chips Act aligned

Where we're headed

The near-term plan is straightforward: grow a small, senior design team across Eindhoven and Porto, fund it through custom ASIC programs and design services, and use that runway to expand our own product families (Nerva, Ostra, Lumen and Vault), each eventually run as an independent business unit. Nerva N2 and Vault M4 are planned for production by 2028. We are optimising for first-pass silicon success and long customer relationships, not for headcount.
