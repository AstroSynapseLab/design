Who we are

Korvane Silicon B.V., registered at Kalkovenweg 117, 5652 RT Eindhoven, Netherlands ("Korvane", "we", "us"), is the data controller for personal data collected through this website, our sample and evaluation-kit requests, and the Tessel Studio desktop app.

What we collect

  • Contact and enquiry data: name, work email, company, expected annual volume and the project description submitted through our contact form.
  • Sample and kit requests: shipping address, company details and the application details needed to qualify for engineering samples.
  • Tessel Studio usage data: crash reports and anonymous feature usage, only when you opt in.
  • Customer design data: specifications, netlists and other design files shared during a design engagement, handled under a separate non-disclosure and data processing agreement.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries and arrange discovery calls.
  • To ship samples and evaluation kits, and to meet export control obligations.
  • To fulfil contractual obligations under design and supply agreements.

Legal basis

  • Consent (newsletters, Tessel Studio telemetry).
  • Contract performance (delivering samples, kits, chips or design services you or your company requested).
  • Legal obligation (export control and product traceability records).
  • Legitimate interest (operating and securing our website).
